After the Revelation, the black sphere changed the human world beyond recognition.

What had once been inorganic began to turn organic. Steel grew bones. Buildings developed flesh and blood. Guns became living things, their structures formed of bone, capable of firing bolts of light.

Humans, too, began to fuse their bodies with these organisms, seeking greater power.

They slept in cocoons, crossed the cosmos in living ships, and grew cities on planet after planet.

In time, humanity reached every corner of the galaxy.

And still the black sphere remained where it had always been.

Until one day, a fissure opened in its surface.

For the first time, people saw what lay within.

A vast golden pupil.

Around it were thousands upon thousands of tiny eyes.

They watched humanity as a god might watch his children.

From that day on, people called it the Creator.

Ten thousand years after the Revelation, humanity founded the greatest state to have existed since the first Homo sapiens walked the earth.

The Holy Galactic Empire.

Our story begins in the year 13576 of the Imperial Calendar.
